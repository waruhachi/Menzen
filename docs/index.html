Menzen

Discord RPC for jailbroken iPhones. Menzen shows what you're listening to, watching or playing on your iPhone in your Discord status, the way Discord does it on desktop.

It works on iOS 14 through 26, on rootful, rootless and RootHide jailbreaks. You sign in with Discord's own sign-in page, and Menzen never asks for your Discord token.

Menzen is free and open source under the GNU GPLv3. The code, releases and issue tracker are on GitHub.

Menzen is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc. or Apple Inc.
