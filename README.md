## About

Menzen is Discord RPC for jailbroken iPhones. It shows what you're listening to, watching or playing on your iPhone in your Discord status, the same way Discord shows it on desktop.

It runs on iOS 14 through 26, on rootful, rootless and RootHide jailbreaks.

## Features

- Shows "Listening to" for music, "Watching" for video and "Playing" for games.
- Built-in support for Apple Music, Apple TV, Spotify, YouTube, YouTube Music, YouTube TV, TIDAL, Deezer, Amazon Music, Pandora, SoundCloud, Qobuz, Audiomack, Netflix, Disney+, Max, Hulu, Prime Video, Paramount+, Pluto TV, Tubi, Crunchyroll, Plex, Twitch and Kick, with album art, thumbnails, episode names and links back to the song or video.
- Podcasts from Apple Podcasts, Castro, Overcast, Pocket Casts and Spotify, with the episode and the show. Apple Podcasts and Spotify also add the artwork and links to the episode.
- Other apps that show up in the Now Playing controls can show up too, with less detail. Browsers, chat apps and social networking apps are always left out.
- Shows the game on screen with its App Store icon, without touching the game itself.
- Shows what the Apelin agent is working on.
- Custom statuses with your own text, pictures and a timer.
- Privacy Mode shows only the app's name and icon.
- Choose which apps and games are allowed to show up.
- Sign in with Discord's official sign-in page on the iPhone, or from another device with a code. Menzen never asks for your Discord token, and the sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain.

## Privacy

Menzen has no servers and no analytics. It only talks to Discord, to GitHub for the app icon list, and to the apps' own services for details like artist pictures. The full [Privacy Policy](https://menzen.waru.moe/privacy/) and [Terms of Service](https://menzen.waru.moe/tos/) are on the website.
